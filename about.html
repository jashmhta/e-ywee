— The atelier · Lisbon, est. 2018

A small clothing house
that moves slowly,
on purpose.

We started ywee as a question — what does a wardrobe look like when every piece is cut by people we know, in cloth we have touched, at a pace that does not fight the cloth itself? Eight years on, we are still answering it.

— The way we work

No shortcuts,
no subcontracting,
no algorithms.

We are six people in a long, narrow studio on Rua das Janelas Verdes — five blocks from the river. Block patterns are drafted here, then sent to the four ateliers we work with. We do not subcontract. We do not auto-generate sizes. Every piece is graded by hand, sampled three times, and signed off by the cutter who made it.

We visit our cloth partners — in Biella, Porto, and Okayama — at the beginning of each season, and we only commission the cloth we have seen on the loom. When a colour or a yarn weight runs out, the garment is closed for the season. We never restock and we never re-issue.

Our pieces are made to be repaired. Every garment carries a small workroom number on the inner spine. If you wear yours through, send it back. We will reweave it, re-finish it, and return it to you, free of charge — for as long as you wear it. Some of our customers are now wearing pieces from our first season. That is the only success that means anything to us.

House timeline

Eight years,
thirty-two collections.

A condensed history of the house. Quieter than most.

2018

The first cuts

Inês and Tomás start ywee in a back room of a textile lab in Almada. Twelve pieces, one cloth partner, seventy-two orders. We cut everything ourselves for the first six months.

2020

Lisbon studio

We move into the long room on Rua das Janelas Verdes. We commission our first pattern grader, Mariana, and welcome Atelier Henriques in Porto as our cutting partner.

2022

The mending program

We commit to lifetime repairs. Customers begin sending pieces back to be rewoven. We hire a head mender, Joana, and outfit a small mending station behind the studio.

2024

Studio editions

We begin numbered, never-re-issued Studio Editions — twelve to twenty pieces a year, often woven from a single one-of-a-kind yardage. They sell to people we have met or written to.

2026
