Mended for free,

Every ywee garment is repaired, rewoven and re-finished by hand — free of charge, for as long as you wear it. We send pre-paid return envelopes from Lisbon every Wednesday.

How it works

Three steps,
no questions asked.

01

Send it back

Email us at mending@ywee.pt with your workroom number (found on the inner spine of your garment). We will send a pre-paid return envelope from Lisbon within 48 hours.

02

We repair by hand

Our head mender, Joana, will assess the damage and repair your garment by hand. Reweaving, re-finishing, re-stitching — whatever it needs. Most repairs take 48 hours.

03

We return it to you

Your garment is returned to you, repaired and ready to wear. No receipt needed, no questions asked, no time limit. As long as you wear it, we will mend it.
