— Garment care

Cloth that
ages with you.

Our garments are made from natural fibres that develop character over time. With proper care, they will last decades. Here is how we recommend caring for each fabric.

By fabric

How to care for
each material.

Wool

WashingDry clean only, or spot clean with cold water. Never machine wash. Wool is naturally odour-resistant — air garments between wears.

StorageStore folded on a shelf, never on a hanger. Use cedar blocks to deter moths. Brush with a soft clothes brush after every few wears.

Cotton & Linen

WashingMachine wash cold on a gentle cycle with mild detergent. Tumble dry low or, better, air dry flat. Iron on medium heat while slightly damp.

StorageHang on wooden or padded hangers. Linen creases are part of the fabric's character — embrace them.

Silk & Viscose

WashingDry clean recommended. For hand washing, use cold water and a gentle detergent. Do not wring — roll in a towel to remove excess water.

StorageHang on padded hangers away from direct sunlight. Avoid perfume contact. Steam rather than iron.

General care

Tips for
longevity.

Between wears

Air garments for 24 hours between wears. Natural fibres need to breathe. This reduces washing frequency and extends garment life.

Stain treatment

Treat stains immediately with cold water. Never rub — blot gently. For oil-based stains, apply talcum powder and leave overnight before brushing off.

Repairs

Small tears and loose buttons should be repaired promptly to prevent further damage. Our mending program covers all repairs, free of charge, for life.

End of life

When a garment has truly reached the end of its life, compost natural fibres (wool, cotton, linen, silk). Viscose and blended fabrics should be recycled through textile programmes.
